    Connect YouTube Music

    Your sign-in stays on the real YouTube Music website.

    1. Install the Jamama Music Companion in Chrome. Download companion ↗
    2. Leave this dialog open. Click the Jamama extension icon and choose Connect to open Jamama. No code copying is needed.
    3. Open a playlist on YouTube Music, click the extension icon, and choose Sync playlist. Join a Jam in the sidebar to request songs.
